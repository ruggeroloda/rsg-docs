_Claude Fable, 20 Nov 2026, from the brief in `README.md`. Working notes and scripts: `analysis/` (`STAGE-1`, `STAGE-2`, `a*.py`, `g*.py`, `s*.sql`). "Visits" = GA4 Google-search sessions; "clicks" = Search Console web search. 200k is read here as Google-search visits a month (Oct 2026: 82.6k). **Correction, Ruggero 20 Nov after reading: 200k means € revenue a year.** The traffic findings stand; section 3 answers the visits question only, the revenue question is the next step (`ROADMAP.md` #16)._

## Answer in five lines
1. No period shows a group of pages that was penalised. Every loss hit all page types and ages about equally, so rewriting individual pages is not what reverses it.
2. From Oct 2025 to Oct 2026 about three quarters of the lost clicks were lost at an unchanged rank (the results page answers first). One quarter is lost rank, nearly all on "[shoe] review" searches.
3. Getting the Oct 2025 ranks back at today's click-through would bring roughly 130k visits a month, not 200k.
4. Comparisons already rank in the top 3 and still lost 64 % of their clicks: refreshing them cannot pay back in Google clicks.
5. The gap to 200k has to come from demand RSG does not hold today ("best" searches, new models) or from outside Google search. Neither is tested yet.

## 3. The path to 200k
- **Where the last year's clicks went** (mobile, searches visible in Search Console, Oct 2025 → Oct 2026): "review" 23.9k → 6.0k (56 % lost rank, 44 % click-through); " vs " 16.7k → 6.0k (97 % click-through); model names 25.0k → 8.8k (89 % click-through).
- **Reachable by ranking:** about 12k of those clicks; scaled to the whole site about +45k visits a month → roughly 130k. That assumes click-through stops falling; it fell every quarter since Aug 2025.
- **Who holds the rest:** in the 4 searches checked, RunRepeat is the first result on 3, Reddit is in the top 3 on all 4, the AI Overview names Believe in the Run, YouTube channels and small blogs. On "best running shoes": RunRepeat, Runner's World, Reddit; RSG's lists sit at position 16 with 278 clicks a month.
- **What a new review earns now:** the Pegasus 42 review (2026) brings about 1,000 visits a month. Eight missing models ≈ 5–8k a month: worth doing, far from the gap.
- **So:** 200k from Google search with today's page types is not supported by this data. It needs either the "best" searches back (lost in 2023, now a brand/RunRepeat/Reddit space) or visits that do not depend on a Google click.

## 4. Rejected, and what would settle the open points
- **"The helpful content update did it":** the Sep 2023 window is the smallest of the three 2023 steps.
- **"Discover is hiding in the numbers":** spikes are ≤ 4 % of weekly visits.
- **"Structured data on 2 Dec 2025":** rich results fell in proportion to everything else.
- **"The ad switch in Feb 2024":** the archive dates it to Jul 2024.
- **"AI Overviews caused May 2025":** the drop starts before the expansion and hits the US, which already had them. Open, not rejected.
- **Would settle:** (1) one month of SISTRIX or Semrush for RSG, RunRepeat, Believe in the Run, Doctors of Running: who gained on each of RSG's dates, and RunRepeat's real size; (2) ad load on RSG's 2023 pages from the archive (blocked today by its rate limit); (3) sources for mid-May 2025; (4) what happened on 6–9 Feb 2024; (5) Raptive and affiliate revenue by page type, to know what a visit to each type is worth.

## Limits
SERP check: 4 searches, desktop, from the Netherlands with US settings. Search Console positions before and after Sep 2025 are not comparable on desktop, so mobile is used. No year-before data for 2023. Competitor claims rest on 4 result pages only.

## Follow-up, 20 Nov evening (conversation with Ruggero after the report)
**Targets (Ruggero):** €200k a year is the hyper-ambitious figure. "Very happy" at about 5k net a month, "amazing" at 10k. Money is parked until the traffic step is done. Affiliate income is currently about half of ad income for the same page views (his estimate); ads were $18.2k in the year to 10 Nov 2026 (`ads/BASELINE-2026-11.md`), so the site earns roughly $1.65k a month, about $13 per 1,000 visits.

**RunRepeat (his question: how do they get 3–5M sessions):** third-party estimates 5.0M visits (Semrush, Jun 2026; 64 % Google organic, 27 % direct) to 7.6M (Similarweb, Nov 2025; 3.4 pages a visit). Eight shoe categories (running, hiking, sneakers, training, walking, tennis, basketball, track) plus a Spanish version; every shoe bought, wear-tested, cut in half, 30+ measurements. Running-only review sites sit far lower in the same estimates: Believe in the Run 273k–572k, Doctors of Running 288k, The Run Testers 294k. RSG: ~130k sessions (Oct 2026).

**What new content adds directly** (`analysis/a11.py`; Google visits a page earns per month at today's level, from what pages of each age earned in Sep–Oct 2026):
| Page | Months 1–6 | Months 7–12 | Year 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Review (few 2026 samples: Pegasus 42 1,040, Cumulus 28 306) | ~400–500 | ~135 | ~100 |
| Comparison | ~270 | ~270 | ~150 |

After 12 months of publishing, with one comparison per review: 1 review a month ≈ +6–7k visits a month, 2 ≈ +13k, 3 ≈ +20k (reviews alone: +3.5k per monthly review). A second year adds about +3k per monthly review. Top models earn about double the review part. Against that, the pages that exist today went from 134k (Mar 2026) to 66k (Oct 2026) visits a month.
Not in this estimate: whether steady publishing lifts the whole site's rank. RSG published about 76 reviews in 2025 and still lost half its Google visits, so cadence alone did not protect it.

**Reskin against what the updates are said to punish** (live pages fetched 20 Nov): the Pegasus 42 review carries a verdict, who should / should not buy, testing details, "Why you can trust us", a named reviewer, "Bought" label, pros and cons, a price comparison and Product/Review/FAQ data; the Clifton vs Bondi comparison has a score breakdown, "Which one to buy", a named writer and "We buy". Ad density is below Raptive's "medium". Thin `/overview/` pages are gone. Not on the pages: measured data of the kind the first-ranked site publishes. Not checked: phone layout and how much of the first screen is ads/price box; the 2023 templates (archive blocked).
