# Raptive history — Nov 2025 to 18 Nov 2026 (read 21 Nov 2026)

Source: Raptive dashboard exports (Ruggero, 21 Nov), raw files and scripts in `_work/raptive-2026-11/` (not in git).
"Human" = Raptive's monetized traffic (earnings ÷ monetized page RPM). Use it, not total page views: from Nov 2026 bots
add 10–60 % to Raptive's page views (e.g. 13 Nov: 14.8k page views, 19 % of sessions monetized) and they earn nothing.
Per-device page views in Raptive include those bots (desktop share 38 % → 55 %), so per-device page RPM is not comparable over time.
