# Shoe list for the next 6 months — draft, 20 Nov 2026

_Work list item 2 of `CONTENT-REVIEW-2026-11-20.md`. Draft for Ruggero to choose from; nothing decided yet._

## Method
- **Demand:** Search Console queries, last 90 days (to ~17 Nov), queries naming the model (`rsg.gsc_query_day`, partial table: good for ranking shoes against each other, not for totals). Monthly Aug–Nov shows the trend. Raw exports (Mac only, not in git): `_work/gsc_queries_90d.tsv`, `_work/gsc_queries_monthly.tsv`.
- **Comparison potential:** impressions of "X vs Y" queries naming the model + whether we reviewed its predecessor (needed for the "new vs previous" comparison, our best-converting type).
- **Our coverage:** review and comparison URLs from the live sitemap (20 Nov).
- **Release status:** checked on the web only for Megablast 2, Superblast 3, Clifton 11, Adios Pro Evo 3, Novablast 6 / Kayano 33 (ASICS Rise & Shine collection, Sep 2026). Ghost 18, Pegasus Plus 2, Winflo 12 and Mach 7 appear in other sites' 2026 reviews. Others: not checked.
- **Blind spot:** Search Console only shows queries Google already shows us for. A shoe we have nothing on (Endorphin Azura, Hyperboost Edge, Cloudsurfer Max 2) shows ~0 here even if the market searches for it. A keyword tool would fill this gap.
- **Site search (Umami, human sessions, 19–20 Nov):** 12 "not tested" searches in total (Novablast 6 ×2; Pegasus 43, Superblast 3, Supernova Rise 3, Adistar 5, Torin 9, Launch 12/13, Revolution 8, Gel Venture 10/11, Revel v5 ×1 each). Too few to rank on; used only as a tiebreak.
