# Core-update analysis, stage 1: where RSG lost search traffic (Fable, 20 Nov 2026)

_Interim working notes, not the report. Numbers are GA4 Google-search visits per week (landing pages, `rsg.ga4_landing_day`), 3–4 weeks before vs after. Scripts in this folder (`a1`–`a8.py`, `s*.sql`; page types by URL in `a6.py`, not `rsg.guess_page_type`). No cause is attributed yet: waiting for Ruggero's timeline of RSG's own changes._

## Loss periods
| | When (first day visible) | Per week before → after | Where the loss sits |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 6 Sep – 27 Oct 2023 (three steps: ~6 Sep, ~14 Sep, 11–23 Oct) | 232k → 154k (−33 %) | old reviews −47 % (41 % of the loss), reviews −23 %, guides −62 %, articles −40 %; US −37 % vs UK −22 % (Aug → Oct) |
| B | 6–9 Feb 2024 | 156k → 134k (−14 %) | reviews 72 % of the loss; all countries; Direct fell 15–20 % the same days; no Google update on those dates |
| C | 6–10 May 2024 step (−12 %), then a slide to Oct 2024 | 147k → 87k (−40 %) | old reviews −57 %, reviews −44 %, comparisons −23 %; `/overview/` pages disappear end of Jul 2024 |
| D | 15 May – 30 Jun 2025, gradual | 85k → 52k (−38 %) | every page type −35 to −46 %; +11 % back from 7 Jul 2025 |
| E | Aug – Nov 2025, gradual | 58k → 44k (−25 %) | comparisons −32 %, listings −45 %, reviews −21 % |
| F | 2–5 Dec 2025 (core update started 11 Dec) | 44k → 34k (−21 %, Nov vs Jan) | reviews −30 % (56 % of the loss) |
| G | from 20 May 2026 | 35k → 25k (−29 %) | comparisons −32 %, reviews −30 % |
| H | Sep – Oct 2026, gradual | 22.6k → 17.4k (−23 %) | comparisons −32 % (51 % of the loss) |

Guides (best-of lists) fell earlier and separately: ~12k/week (Mar 2023) → 6.9k in the week of 3 Jul 2023 → 3.7k after 4 Sep 2023 → ~50 today.

## New pages earn less, in two steps (`a8.py`)
Median Google-search visits of a review in its first 13 weeks, by quarter published: 2023 Q2–Q4 4,200–4,600 · 2024 Q1 3,100 · 2024 Q2 – 2025 Q3 1,800–2,200 (flat for six quarters) · 2025 Q4 460. New comparisons held 2,400–3,700 until 2025 Q3, then 750 (2025 Q4) and 440 (2026 Q1).
