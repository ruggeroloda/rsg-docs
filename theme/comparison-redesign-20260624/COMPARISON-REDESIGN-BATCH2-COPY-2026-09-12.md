## 1. asics-superblast-vs-asics-novablast-3 — Superblast 2 vs Novablast 5 (3,287 clicks)

**Verdict:** Two ASICS max-cushioned trainers with a similar bouncy ride — the difference is
versatility. The Superblast 2 is built for faster paces and racing; the Novablast 5 is the more
stable, more durable daily trainer for easy-to-moderate runs.

**Buy the FIRST shoe (Superblast 2) if:** your runs skew faster, you want a shoe that doubles as
a racer, and you don't mind paying more for a lighter, more breathable upper.

**Buy the SECOND shoe (Novablast 5) if:** you want a cheaper, more stable daily trainer for
easy-to-moderate runs and value outsole durability over race-day speed.
