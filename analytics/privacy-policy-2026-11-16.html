This page explains what information Running Shoes Guru ("we", "us") collects when you use runningshoesguru.com, why we collect it, who helps us process it, and the choices you have. If you have any question, write to us at guru@runningshoesguru.com.

The short version

  • You can read the whole site without giving us your name or email address.
  • Our own analytics run on our own server in Germany, use no cookies and do not store your IP address.
  • We do not record visitors' sessions (no "session replay").
  • We earn money from ads (through Raptive) and from commissions when you buy something through a shop link on our site. Those partners have their own privacy policies, linked below.
  • If you sign up for our emails, we use your address only to send you what you asked for, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

When you visit the site

Hosting and security. Our website is hosted by Rocket.net and delivered through Cloudflare's network. Like every website, their servers receive your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, so that the pages can be delivered and the site protected against abuse.

Our own analytics (Umami). We use Umami, an open-source analytics tool, running on our own server at Hetzner in Germany. It tells us which pages are read, how people find the site (for example a search engine or a link), and how our price boxes and shop links are used, so that we can make the site more useful. For each visit it records the pages viewed, the referring website, browser and operating system, device type, screen size, language, page loading speed, technical errors, and interactions with our price boxes (for example which box was seen or which shop link was clicked). Your IP address is used for a moment to work out your country, region and city and is then discarded; it is never stored. To count visitors without cookies, Umami creates an anonymous identifier from your IP address and browser details mixed with a secret value that changes every month, so you cannot be followed from one month to the next. For a small share of visits from outside the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we also record where on a page people click and how far they scroll, to build "heatmaps" of page use; these are not recordings of your session. Umami data is not shared with anyone and is kept for up to three years.

You can switch our analytics off in your browser at any time: turn off Running Shoes Guru analytics in this browser (and turn it back on). The setting is stored in your browser, so it applies to that browser and device only.

Google Analytics. We also use Google Analytics 4, which sets cookies, to measure the performance of the site. Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Local prices. To show prices from shops in your country, the site asks Cloudflare for your country code when a page with prices loads. If you choose a country or "men"/"women" in a price box, that choice is remembered in your browser's local storage. Our own log of price-box views and shop clicks records the page, the price box, the shop and the country code; it contains no IP address and no cookie, and is kept for up to three years.

Product images. Some shoe images are loaded directly from Amazon's servers, which therefore receive your IP address and browser details.

Shop links and commissions

Many links to shops on our site are affiliate links: if you buy something after clicking one, we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. We also work with affiliate networks such as Awin, CJ and Rakuten, and directly with shops. When you click such a link you go to the shop's or network's website, which may set its own cookies to record that the sale came from us; their privacy policies apply there. The codes we add to these links describe the page and the price box you clicked from and the country shown; they contain no personal information about you.

To learn more about Raptive's data usage, click here: https://www.raptive.com/publisher-advertising-privacy-policy

Where the law requires it, you are asked for your consent before advertising cookies are used, and you can change your choices through the privacy settings link shown on the site.

When you give us information

Newsletter and running guides. If you sign up for our emails or download one of our guides, we store your email address (and anything else you choose to give us) with MailerLite, the service we use to send our newsletters. In the EU, the EEA and Switzerland we ask you to confirm your subscription by email first. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and we keep your address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

Shoe Finder. If you enter your email address in the Shoe Finder, we store it together with your country, a summary of your answers, the three shoes we picked for you and the IP address of the request (as a record of your sign-up). We email you your picks through MailerSend and, as explained next to the form, add you to our newsletter in MailerLite (in the EU, the EEA and Switzerland only after you confirm by email). Separately, answers given in the Shoe Finder are counted without any personal information to improve the tool.

Contact form. Messages sent through our contact form reach us by email with the name and email address you entered. We use them only to answer you and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Comments. If you leave a comment, we store the name, email address and IP address submitted with it, as WordPress does, and the comment itself. Comments are checked for spam by Akismet (Automattic). Your email address is never published.

Why we may use your information (EU and UK visitors)

We rely on our legitimate interest in running, securing and improving the site and in measuring how it is used (hosting, our own analytics, price boxes, spam protection); on your consent for our newsletter and, where required, for advertising cookies; and on legal obligations where they apply.

Where your information is stored

Our analytics data is stored in Germany. Our website is hosted in the United States, and several of the services named above are based in the United States or process data there; they use the safeguards required by law for such transfers. Our backups are encrypted.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to object to or limit how we use it, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Write to guru@runningshoesguru.com and we will answer within one month. If you are in the EU or the UK you can also complain to your data protection authority.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Our advertising partner may use data to show ads based on your interests; you can opt out of this through the privacy settings link shown on the site and through Raptive's policy linked above.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
