# Video b-roll shot list (per pair)

_Draft 19 Nov 2026 (Ruggero + Claude), not agreed yet. Context: YouTube restart idea; Brandon emailed 19 Nov 2026, no answer yet. This is step 1 of 5 (b-roll spec → a-roll per format → section-to-shot mapping → file naming and handover → pilot with one pair)._

## Principle (Ruggero, 19 Nov)
- **B-roll is filmed once per pair and reused everywhere:** long review, comparisons, horizontal and vertical videos. **Only the a-roll (the talking) changes per format.**
- Comparisons don't need both pairs filmed together: they are built in the edit (side by side or one after the other). That only works if every pair is filmed in **exactly the same set-up** (section "Fixed set").
- One horizontal 4K shoot also gives the vertical version: the shoe stays in the **middle third** of the frame, and a vertical crop out of 4K is still full HD.

## How much footage this gives
| Block | Shot codes | Raw | Usable (estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product | P01–P15 | ~5.5 min | ~3.8 min |
| On foot | F01–F04 | ~1.1 min | ~0.7 min |
| Running | R01–R07 | ~2.7 min | ~1.3 min (incl. slow motion) |
| Wear (after ~100 km) | W01–W03 | ~0.9 min | ~0.6 min |
| **Total** | **29 shots** | **~10 min** | **~6.5 min** |

- A 4-minute review needs **~170 s of b-roll** (about 70 % of the runtime, a cut every 3–5 s = 40–55 clips). The library gives about twice that, so a review doesn't use every clip, and Shorts and comparisons get fresh ones.
- "Usable" = what is left after trimming shaky starts, hands in frame, focus hunting. Product shots ~70 %, on foot ~60 %, running only 2–3 s per pass with the shoe in frame.
- **Filming time:** product + on foot ~35–45 min (incl. setting up), running ~20–30 min, wear ~10 min (a later day). Not "15 minutes".

## Fixed set (what makes comparisons possible)
- **Same place every time:** same table or board as surface, same plain background, same spot for running (a quiet path with even light).
- **Tape marks** on the floor/table for the tripod positions:
  - **A** eye level, three-quarter view, ~50 cm from the shoe
  - **B** overhead (top-down), straight down
  - **C** ground level, side view
- **Same light:** indirect daylight at the same time of day, or one LED panel in the same place. No mixed light.
- **Camera locked:** 4K, 30 fps (120 fps only for slow motion), main lens at 1× (no digital zoom), exposure, focus and white balance locked per shot.
- **Horizontal only**, shoe in the middle third of the frame (the vertical crop zone).
- **Same shoe for single shots:** always the right shoe, laced the same way, insole in, clean.
- **Product shots on unboxing day, before the first run.** Wear shots (W) repeat the same positions after ~100 km.
- **No talking during b-roll.** Ambient sound is fine; music is added in the edit.
- "Static" = camera doesn't move. "Move" = one slow, even move (slide, push-in or turn), start and end still for 2 s.

## Kit
- Camera: phone with 4K (fine) or camera. _To ask Brandon._
- Tripod with a phone/camera clamp; tape for the marks.
- For the moves: small slider or phone gimbal (handheld moves look shaky when cut together).
- Small motorised turntable (for P09).
- Kitchen scale with a display (P13).
- Lapel mic: a-roll only.
