# Nike Pegasus 42 — social scripts (5 formats)

All content pulled from the live RSG review. Recurring style anchors baked in:
**shot on the RSG deck** · signature sign-off **"we bought this with our own money"** ·
**honest, unafraid-to-say-skip** voice.

Source facts used: MSRP $145 (seen at $107) · 10.3 oz · 10 mm drop · 38 mm/28 mm stack · no plate ·
runs small, narrow toebox · ReactX feels flat/firm, no rocker · new full-length Zoom Air fixed the
41's lumpy forefoot but adds no bounce · one of the heaviest daily trainers (Clifton 10, Ride 19,
Cumulus 28, Ghost 17 all lighter) · scores: Comfort 8, Build 9, Upper 9, Sole 6, Landing 6,
Transition 6, Toe-off 6, Traction 8, Durability 9, Value 6 · reviewer Brandon Law.

> ⚠️ Verify-before-posting: in the vs script, the "Clifton is softer / rockered / wears faster" lines
> are Clifton's known character, not from *this* test. Fine to say, but say it as general knowledge.

## 1. THE VERDICT — hero short (~25–30s, vertical, host-to-camera or VO)

**HOOK (0–2s, hold the shoe):**
"Nike sells millions of these a year. I still don't think most runners should buy it."

**BODY:**
- "This is the Pegasus 42 — Nike's daily trainer. $145, though I've seen it at $107."
- "The good news: the new full-length Zoom Air finally killed the lumpy forefoot from the 41."
- "The bad news: the ReactX foam underneath it feels five years old. Flat, firm, no rocker."
- "And at 10.3 ounces it's one of the *heaviest* daily trainers you can buy — a Clifton, a Ride, a
  Cumulus are all lighter and livelier."
- "Where it wins? Build. It's a tank — 9 out of 10 — with a genuinely great upper and grippy outsole.
  For easy miles and walking, it'll last for years."

**BUY/SKIP (the turn):**
"So — daily beater you'll never wear out? Buy it. A shoe you're *excited* to run in? Skip it."

**FIT TAG (throwaway, 1s):** "Oh, and it runs small. Size up half."

**SIGN-OFF:** "We bought this with our own money. Full review on Running Shoes Guru."

*Caption:* The most popular running shoe on earth… is kind of a walking shoe now. Pegasus 42 verdict 👇
*On-screen text beats:* $145 (seen $107) · "one of the heaviest" · BUILD 9/10 · "SKIP if you want fun"

## 2. WHO SHOULD **NOT** BUY THIS — (~18s, faceless VO + b-roll)

**HOOK (0–2s):** "Four runners who should NOT buy the Nike Pegasus 42."

1. "If you're a forefoot or midfoot striker — the tall 10 mm drop and firm forefoot fight you."
2. "If you've got wide feet — the toebox is genuinely narrow. Size up, or get the wide."
3. "If you want energy return — the ReactX and Zoom Air give you almost none. It hates picking up
   the pace."
4. "If you want *light* — 10.3 ounces, heavier than the Clifton 10, Ride 19, Cumulus 28 and Ghost 17."

**TURN:** "Still one of the best-built shoes out there, though. Who SHOULD buy it? Link in bio."
**SIGN-OFF:** "Bought with our own money."

*Caption:* Great shoe. Wrong buyers. Save someone the $145 👇
*On-screen text:* one number per point — 10mm · NARROW · 0 bounce · 10.3oz

## 3. PEGASUS 42 vs HOKA CLIFTON 10 — (~30s, mix: VO over split b-roll)

**HOOK (0–2s, one in each hand):** "Nike Pegasus 42 versus Hoka Clifton 10. Same money, opposite shoes."

**RAPID COMPARE (name the winner each beat):**
- **Weight →** "Clifton. The Peg is one of the heaviest trainers going at 10.3 oz."
- **Ride →** "Clifton. It's soft and rolls you forward. The Peg is firm and flat — no rocker."
- **Excitement →** "Clifton. The Peg feels familiar; the Clifton feels like it wants to move."
- **Durability →** "Pegasus. That outsole's a tank — 9/10 build. Cliftons wear quicker."
- **Fit →** "Careful. The Peg runs small and narrow — size up half."

**VERDICT:** "Soft and springy for easy miles? Clifton. A bombproof shoe that lasts and looks sharp?
Pegasus — just don't expect it to be fun."
**SIGN-OFF:** "Both bought with our own money. Full comparison on RSG."

*Caption:* $145 each. Completely different shoes. Which one's yours? 👇
*On-screen text:* WEIGHT ▸ Clifton · RIDE ▸ Clifton · DURABILITY ▸ Peg · scoreboard feel

### Reuse notes
- Every script ends on the same sign-off = your audio/brand signature.
- Same deck/surface + same text-card style across all 5 = your visual signature.
- Formats 1–3 are the reach engine; 4 is zero-filming; 5 is the b-roll showcase that tells you
  exactly what to capture. Shoot Format 5's five shots + a talking take = you have all five pieces
  from one ~30-min session.
