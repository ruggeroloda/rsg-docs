What to photograph for every shoe you test. The photos are from our Nike Winflo 11 review and show what we are after. Where there is no example yet, a sketch shows the angle. Where you shoot and how it looks is yours.

Before you start

  • Outdoors. Shade, an overcast day or low sun. No midday sun, no flash. A real place in the background is welcome as long as the shoe stands out from it.
  • Landscape first. Every shot in landscape for the website. In use and Beauty shots also in portrait, for social.
  • Room around the shoe. Shoe in the middle, sharp, with some space on every side.
  • Two or three of each. Change the angle or distance a little each time. We pick the best one.
  • Send the originals. Full size, straight from the phone. No filters, no edits. Main lens for the numbered shots.
  • Tidy shoe. Laces neat or tucked in. If the upper sags, stuff it with the paper from the box.

The set

All 11, on day one. Before the first run, while the shoe is clean. These go with the sections of the review, and number 1 is the main image of the post.

Details

At least three, and you find them. Every shoe has small things no other shoe has. Get very close, and send one line with each photo saying what it is.

Where to look: an etched or embossed logo, a visible air unit or plate, words on the heel tab, the lace tips, a print on the insole, a reflective strip, the texture of the foam, stitching or a seam, a pattern in the outsole, a tech name printed on the midsole.

These are the ones we found on the Winflo 11. Your shoe will have different ones.

In use

At least three. Proof the shoe was run in. Landscape and portrait. A tripod with the timer, or someone to press the button, helps for the running one.

Beauty

Free: as many as you like. Your eye, your place. These rotate as main images and go on social, so at least one in portrait. What the best ones have in common: the camera sits on the ground, the shoe fills most of the frame, and the background is far behind it and soft. Portrait mode or the 2× lens helps here.

After testing

All five, at the end. Same angle as the day-one shot, so before and after can sit side by side. Do not clean the shoe first.
